# Logo Size Update - Made Even Smaller

## 📏 **New Logo Sizes Applied**

### **Before vs After Comparison**

| Page | Previous Size | New Size | Reduction |
|------|---------------|----------|-----------|
| **Login Page** | `h-16 w-16` (64px) | `h-12 w-12` (48px) | 25% smaller |
| **Navigation Bar** | `h-8 w-8` (32px) | `h-6 w-6` (24px) | 25% smaller |
| **Input Page Header** | `w-12 h-12` (48px) | `w-10 h-10` (40px) | 17% smaller |

## ✅ **Changes Applied**

### **1. Login Page (First Page)**
```jsx
// BEFORE
<img className="h-16 w-16 object-contain" />

// AFTER  
<img className="h-12 w-12 object-contain" />
```
- **Result**: Much smaller, professional logo at center of login page

### **2. Navigation Bar**
```jsx
// BEFORE
<div className="h-8 w-8 bg-white rounded-lg">

// AFTER
<div className="h-6 w-6 bg-white rounded-lg">
```
- **Result**: Minimal, clean logo in top-left corner

### **3. Input Page Header**
```jsx
// BEFORE
<div className="w-12 h-12 bg-white rounded-xl">

// AFTER
<div className="w-10 h-10 bg-white rounded-lg">
```
- **Result**: Compact logo that doesn't dominate the header

## 🎯 **Final Result**

The logos are now **very small and professional** across all pages:

- ✅ **Login Page**: Small 48px logo - doesn't overwhelm the form
- ✅ **Navigation**: Tiny 24px logo - subtle branding
- ✅ **Input Page**: Compact 40px logo - professional header accent

## 📱 **Visual Impact**

- **Professional Appearance**: Logos are now appropriately sized for business application
- **Better Balance**: Text and content are the focus, not the logo

- **Clean Interface**: Logos provide subtle brand presence without domination

The application now has **very small, professional logos** that enhance rather than dominate the interface!
